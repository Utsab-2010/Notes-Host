---
title: "Johnson–Lindenstrauss lemma"
lastmod: 2026-09-05
---

The [lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemma_\(mathematics\) "Lemma (mathematics)") states that a set of points in a high-dimensional space can be embedded into a space of much lower dimension in such a way that distances between the points are [nearly preserved](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stretch_factor "Stretch factor"). In the classical proof of the lemma, the embedding is a [random](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random "Random") [orthogonal projection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_projection "Orthogonal projection").
