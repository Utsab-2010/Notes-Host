Hi Utsab.
Today I want to write about a person whose story deeply moved me. 
John Hudson. A kid about my age who had been battleling with an awful disease called EB (Epidermolysis Bullosa (EB) is **a family of life-threatening rare genetic disorders that affect the body's largest organ: the skin**.)

I came across his story through the yt channel called "special books by special kids". John was always in so much pain, yet you could see his strength and character which seemed to over his situation. He contributed to helping so many people through his story and contributions towards EB research.

Watching the video honestly broke me . I bawled my eyes out even though I had become quite tolerant to stuff like these. John's strength of charcter moved my core. He was a beacon of light and courage, in this otherwise dark world.

I wanted to write about him here so that maybe in the future when I am having a tough time, I can look back and draw inspiration from him.

John Hudson passed away in April of this year. May his soul rest in peace and I hope he is getting the rest he needed. 

It has been a while since I have been moved like this. Really makes you question the amount of strength a human being can have. John was an example of that. I will try to remember John. This feels very performative but I hope in whatever little memory I have I can carry a bit of John's memory too and draw strength from it.

Fly high John Hudson.
