I am watching a video on why I feel sleepy when I start studying.
 - Its not diet, exercise or energy deficiency. It is a more emotional issue.
- What is your brain predicting when you sit down to study?
- Some emotions protect you from other emotions.
	- Anger
		- Anger protects you from shame, you go defensive for your ego.
	- Frustration
- Similarly tiredness is a secondary emotion. 
- Your brain is predicting that something negative is going to get triggered when you start studying.
	- One common thing is frustration about not being able to understand smth, comparision with others, being shamed,etc.
- Some self-deceptions:
	- Time blindness
	- Thinking about other rando stuff we should do, like going for a walk or going for some sunlight . e.g I wanna get some exercise done rn , cant study becoz haven't done that in quite some time.
- Tiredness is an entity that takes over you(not really but think of it that way).
	- The more you allow it to take control, the stronger it gets.
	- Start working on your emotions. See where the tiredness if coming for. Convince yourself otherwise. 
	- Emotional Mastery.
- Next time you start getting tired do this:
	- Instead of telling yourself that you need to get 100 different things done , just start with a small step.
		- Maybe reading one paragraph.
		- Maybe writing the code for one experiment.
		- Maybe taking notes for one paper.
		- Maybe reading 5 pages of notes.
