The people pleasing says "I need everyone to approve of me." The ego says "I'm more mature than everyone." Both are ways of keeping people at a distance. One through over-inclusion, one through quiet superiority. Neither lets real connection in.

The advice is the same for both:

**Get curious instead of comparative.** Instead of pre-deciding someone can't handle something, get genuinely curious about them. You might be surprised. The "I'm more mature" feeling fades when you actually discover depth in people you'd written off.

**Let people surprise you.** Plan the trip with who you actually want. See what happens. Nobody will die. Most won't even notice. And the ones who do — their reaction will tell you something useful about that friendship.

**Notice when you're managing people vs connecting with them.** Managing is exhausting and keeps you lonely. Connecting is scary but actually feeds you.
