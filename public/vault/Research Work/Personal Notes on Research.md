- If you can't Claude, you GPT for summary and discussion compared to Gemini. I find the overall experience to be much more pleasant. Without Gemini, it feels more liike a conversation with a chronic gaslighter.
- Start with LLM-generated broad summary of the content. Then familiarise yourself with the main ideas, new jargon and key assumptions.
	- Following this proceed with reading the text. While this feels like a surrender to AI, it is not. You are still reading and pondering, just makes the information extraction faster. 
- Brainstorm and discuss with LLMs. Discussions with people and field experts are the fastest way to gain clarity regarding a topic. It also helps you remember important points, connect things to previous reading sessions and papers. Once again this ain't cheating, its a tool meant for you to use. The more you brainstorm ideas , the more clear you will be regarding your research question.
- Use it to get familiar sources(gpt better than gemini in this) and counter examples. Also use it to generate possible research directions, think about it , extrapolate on its intital ideas and experiment.
- After experiments, discuss the results. Sitting on your ass and just looking at the plots and trying to conjure up an explaination will take you ages especially when its a field where you don't have years of expertise. You don't have to prove to anyone that you are a prodigy. You ain't that person bro. Just get the job done, you'll be much more happy when you have actually formulated a good theory for your work and gotten results.

> LLMs and chatbots are more like a better search engine. You are sitll doing the same thing, it just makes the process a LOT faster. It also democratises research power to the common people. Anyone can get into anything, learn well using these and maybe even go into research. The time to attain expertise has drastically reduced.
