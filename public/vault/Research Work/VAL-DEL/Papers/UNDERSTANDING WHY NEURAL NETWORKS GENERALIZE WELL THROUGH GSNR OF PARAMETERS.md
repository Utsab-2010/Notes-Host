- Intuitively, GSNR measures the similarity of a parameter’s gradients among different training samples. Large GSNR implies that most training samples agree on the optimization direction of this parameter, thus the parameter is more likely to be associated with a meaningful “pattern” and we assume its update could lead to a better generalization
